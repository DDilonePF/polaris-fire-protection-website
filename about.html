About Us

Who We Are

One Team. Every Phase of Fire Protection. Life Safety.

Polaris Fire Protection is a full-service Fire Sprinkler contractor headquartered in Fort Lauderdale, Florida with offices and operations extending throughout the state and the Mid-Atlantic region of the eastern United States (MD/DC/VA).

We provide complete Fire Protection services, including the engineering, design and installation of Fire Protection systems for all types of new and existing construction, from multi-story high rises to commercial, industrial, and retail developments.

In Florida, as the fastest growing fire protection contractor in the state, Polaris has earned the trust of top construction firms and developers by focusing on accurate communication, competitive pricing, on time execution, and unmatched quality control.

Our greatest strength is our people: expert field managers, dedicated installers, and industry leading design professionals who bring quality and value to every project.

Since 2006 we have been driven by our commitment to safety, integrity, and excellence, Polaris continues to set a new standard in Fire Protection.
