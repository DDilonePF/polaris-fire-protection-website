Work Rules

Please read all 30 work rules below, then acknowledge at the bottom.

  1. All tools and equipment shall be maintained in good condition.
  2. Damaged tools or equipment shall be removed from service and tagged "DEFECTIVE".
  3. Only appropriate tools shall be used for the job.
  4. Wrenches shall not be altered by the addition of handle extensions or "cheaters".
  5. Portable electric tools shall not be lifted or lowered by means of power cord. Use ropes if needed.
  6. Only authorized personnel shall operate machinery or equipment.
  7. Loose or frayed clothing, long hair, dangling ties, finger rings, etc., shall not be worn around moving machinery or other sources of entanglement.
  8. Machinery shall not be serviced, repaired or adjusted while in operation, nor shall oiling of moving parts be attempted, except on equipment designed with safeguards.
  9. Use hand carts, mechanical stock handling equipment, or other workers when moving heavy loads.
  10. Consult MSDSs if you do not know the hazards associated with a particular chemical.
  11. Foremen shall insist on employees observing every rule, regulation, and order necessary for safe conduct of work.
  12. Anyone under the influence of drugs or intoxicating substances shall not be allowed on the job.
  13. Work shall be well planned and supervised to prevent injuries in handling materials and working with equipment.
  14. No one shall be permitted or required to work while impaired by fatigue, illness, or other causes.
  15. Do not enter voids, chambers, tanks, or similar places with poor ventilation unless determined safe by a competent individual.
  16. Do not handle or tamper with electrical equipment, machinery, or utility lines outside the scope of your duties without supervisor instructions.
  17. All injuries shall be reported promptly to the supervisor.
  18. Maintain good housekeeping at all times.
  19. Materials, tools, or objects shall not be thrown from buildings until proper precautions are taken.
  20. Before leaving any job, be sure it is in a safe condition.
  21. Arrange work so employees face the ladder and use both hands while climbing.
  22. Report any damaged scaffolds, false work, or supporting structures immediately.
  23. Always place ladders so that side rails have secure footing.
  24. Do not climb higher than the third rung from the top on straight ladders, nor the second tread on step ladders.
  25. Keep appropriate fire extinguishers at or near the work location.
  26. Absolutely no smoking in areas where flammable chemicals are present.
  27. Always use proper respiratory protection when working around paints or dust.
  28. Check with your supervisor for proper PPE: gloves, goggles, protective suits, barrier creams.
  29. Follow all safety precautions on MSDSs and environmental regulations when using chemicals.
  30. Always wear safety belts when operating company vehicles or personal vehicles on company business.
